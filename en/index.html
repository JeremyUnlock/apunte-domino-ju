Apunte Dominó JU

Keep score for your domino games on iPhone: two teams, voidable rounds, history and rankings.

Support

Have a problem or a suggestion? Here you will find answers to the most common questions and how to reach us.

What can I do with Apunte Dominó JU?

  • Keep score for two teams during the game.
  • Type each team's name above its score.
  • Void a round by swiping it, and restore it if you need to.
  • Set how many points are needed to win.
  • See the history of finished games with the details of every round.
  • Check the rankings: wins, losses and win rate for each team, and compare two teams against each other.
  • Export your data to a file to share it with someone else, and import a file that was sent to you.

Do I need an account?

No. The app does not require creating an account or signing in. All of its features are available right away.

Where is my data stored?

Teams, scores, history and rankings are stored only on your device. The app does not use its own server to store that information.

How does the scoreboard work?

The game ends automatically when a team reaches the target score. If, while looking at the results, you void a play and the total drops below the target, the game resumes. Results are filed into the history and the rankings when you tap “New game”.

What are the ads that appear?

The bottom of the scoreboard may show informational banners. These banners are downloaded from a public link and update on their own when you have a connection, with no need to update the app. With no connection, the last saved content is shown.

How do I delete all my data?

In Settings → Data management you can use “Delete all data”. That removes the game history, the teams and the saved rankings on the device. You can also reset the settings to their default values from that same section.

Can I move my data to another phone?

Yes. In Settings → Data management you can export a file with your history, your teams and your rankings, and send it by WhatsApp, email or save it to Files. The other person only has to open Import data and choose that file.

When importing, the app warns you that the file's contents replace all the data stored on the phone, so it is worth keeping that in mind before confirming.

Do you have a problem or a suggestion?

If you find a bug, something that does not work as you expected, or you want to suggest an improvement, write to us saying what happened and, if possible, the steps to reproduce it. It helps us improve the app.

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Apunte Dominó JU is an app for keeping score in domino games. We respect your privacy and designed it to work with storage on your own device, without accounts and without collecting personal data.

2. Information the app collects

Apunte Dominó JU does not collect, transmit or share personal information with servers of its own. We do not use user accounts, profiles, third-party analytics or advertising tracking.

3. Information you enter

The app stores on your device the information you type to keep score, such as:

  • The names of the teams.
  • The points of each round and the voided rounds.
  • The target score needed to win.
  • The history of finished games and the team rankings.

This information is stored locally on the device. If you uninstall the app, that data is deleted with it.

4. Exporting and importing your data

The app can create a backup file with your history, your teams and your rankings, and read it back later. That file is created on your own device and you share it yourself, using the system options (email, messaging, Files or other apps you have installed).

The app does not send that file to any server of its own or to third parties on its own: it is only shared when you decide to. If you send it to someone else or through an external service, that service will handle the file under its own privacy policy, and whoever receives it will be able to see the team names and the results it contains.

When importing a file, its contents replace the data stored on the device.

5. Ads and banners

The app may show informational banners on the scoreboard screen. Their content is downloaded from a public link hosted on GitHub, so it can be updated without releasing a new version of the app.

When those banners are downloaded, your device makes a network request to that service. The app does not send that service any personal data or information about your games; only the content file is requested. The hosting provider (GitHub) may log technical data about the connection, such as the IP address, under its own policies.

If there is no internet connection, the app keeps working normally and shows the last saved content.

6. Third-party services

In its current version, the app does not include any advertising, behavioural analytics or tracking SDK. The only external service that may be used is the public hosting of the banners file described in the previous point.

The app does use Apple technologies that are part of the system, such as local storage on the device and opening web pages in the system browser.

7. Data deletion

From Settings → Data management you can delete all the history, the teams and the rankings, or reset the settings to their default values. You can also delete all data by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Your data is stored on the device. We recommend keeping the system up to date and using the security measures available on iOS, such as the passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

9. Children's privacy

The app is not intended to deliberately collect personal information from children and does not require creating accounts or entering personal data to be used.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when necessary to reflect changes in the app or in its privacy practices. The date shown at the top of this page will be changed when a new version is published.
